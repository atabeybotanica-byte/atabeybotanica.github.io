Your Daily Ritual

The Nourishing Rub Ritual

An invitation to give yourself some of the care you so freely give to others.

We spend so much of our lives caring for the people and things that matter to us. The Nourishing Rub is a simple reminder to include yourself.

Warm the butter between your palms. Close your eyes and take a slow breath.

Warm. Breathe. Nourish.

Massage it into your skin with intention. Move slowly. Notice your hands against your body. Give a little more attention to the places asking for care.

As you move, offer your body a few loving words. Choose the ones that feel true to you.

Choose one, or a few, and let it settle

I am worthy of loving devotion.

I am grateful for all the places my body has carried me today.

I honor my body for all that it does for me.

I offer my body tenderness, patience, and care.

I am grateful to be here, in this body, in this moment.

I choose to speak to myself with love.

I give myself permission to receive care.

I love and appreciate the body that carries me through this life.

Place both hands over your heart. Take one final, slow breath.

Thank you for all you have done, all you have held, and all you continue to do.
I am grateful for you.

Pause. Breathe. Receive.
