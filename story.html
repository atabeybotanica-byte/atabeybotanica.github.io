The Story Behind Atabey Botanica

Where It Began

I founded Atabey Botanica after my cancer journey, when my skin needed extraordinary care and could not tolerate anything synthetic. I needed something pure that the earth made, something my body could receive without fighting.

So I went back to the plants my ancestors had always known: frankincense, shea butter, and cocoa butter. Something happened as I started making these blends. It was not only my skin that began to heal.

I realized I had spent my life caring for everyone else through my career and my family, and had never made a ritual of caring for myself. So I started slowly, with intention and gratitude, using the butter to nurture every part of me with loving attention, until the ritual became my daily self-care, a devotion I could not rush past.

What began as a private return to myself became something I felt called to share, with anyone ready to come back to themselves.

I called it Atabey Botanica, and here is why.
The First Word

Atabey

ah-tah-BAY  ·  the original mother

Across the oldest traditions, there is always a first mother, present before language and the naming of things. Every culture near the earth knew her by a different name. The name changes. The truth beneath it does not.

We carry her name as a reminder of what we strip away: the synthetic, the unnecessary. What remains is the earth as mother, plant wisdom, a body worth tending.

The Second Word

Botanica

bo-TAH-nee-kah  ·  of plants  ·  the world of botanical knowledge

Long before laboratories, people kept botanical knowledge in their hands, passed down as recipe and ritual. Plants were not a backup plan, they were the plan, and still are.

We are not inventing anything. We are remembering.
