Our Story

I founded Atabey Botanica after my cancer journey, when my skin needed extraordinary care and my body could no longer tolerate what once seemed ordinary. I longed for ingredients my body could receive with gentleness, so I returned to the plants my ancestors had trusted for generations.

What I discovered was far greater than a nourishing body butter.

For years, I poured my energy into caring for my career, my family, and everyone around me. Cancer taught me I couldn't rush past my own care anymore. I had to slow down. I had to care for myself with the same devotion I had always given others.

Each day, applying body butter became more than skincare. It became a quiet ritual, a moment to pause, breathe, and reconnect with myself. In that stillness, I learned that self care isn't a luxury. It's non-negotiable.

That daily ritual changed the way I cared for myself, and it became the heart of Atabey Botanica.

Today, every jar is an invitation to pause, nourish your skin, and remember that caring for yourself is one of the greatest gifts you can give yourself.

I called it Atabey Botanica, and here is why.
Atabey
ah-tah-BAY  ·  the original mother

Across the world's oldest traditions, there is always a first mother, present long before language and the naming of things. The names may change, but the truth beneath them does not. We carry her name as a reminder to return to what endures: the wisdom of the earth, the intelligence of plants, and a body worthy of care. By stripping away the synthetic and the unnecessary, we make space for what has always been enough.

Botanica
bo-TAH-nee-kah  ·  of plants  ·  the world of botanical knowledge

Long before laboratories, people kept botanical knowledge in their hands, passed down as recipe and ritual. Plants were not a backup plan, they were the plan, and still are. We are not inventing anything. We are remembering.
