Ingredient Reference

What's in it. Why it's here.

Every ingredient is chosen with purpose, from the plant butters that form our foundation to the botanicals that distinguish each formula. Nothing unnecessary. Nothing hidden.

The Foundation

What every butter begins with

Ingredient
What it brings
Unrefined Shea Butter
Rich plant butter that deeply moisturizes and softens dry skin.
Unrefined Cocoa Butter
A rich emollient that helps soften rough, dry skin and gives our butters their signature body.
Fractionated Coconut Oil
Lightweight plant oil that helps the formula glide easily and absorb without a heavy finish.
Jojoba Oil
Lightweight conditioning oil valued for its compatibility with the skin's natural oils.
Vitamin E
Plant-derived antioxidant that helps protect the formula while conditioning the skin.

These ingredients form the foundation of our body butters. Each expression is then shaped by the botanical chosen for its distinct skin qualities.
